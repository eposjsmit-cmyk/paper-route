# Paper Route

A two-player co-op flying game. Bargain Air sold every cable in the plane, so
the crew fly it by hand: walk out to the flaps, crank the fan, pat out the fires.

**Download (Windows):** get `PaperRoute-portable.zip` from the
[latest release](../../releases/latest), unzip it anywhere, and double-click
`Play Paper Route.bat`. Nothing to install.

Two players on one PC: two controllers (PlayStation or Xbox) or one keyboard.
Plug the controllers in before you start. The controls are in `README.txt`
inside the zip.
